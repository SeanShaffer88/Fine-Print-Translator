Harborlight Community Guidelines

1. Be respectful
Treat other members with courtesy. Harassment is not permitted.

2. Account security
Renew your password regularly and do not share it with anyone.

3. Uploads
You are free to cancel a pending upload at any time before it finishes.

4. Moderation
Moderators learn what works through trial and error, and decisions may be revised.

5. Performance
The page will refresh periodically to show new posts.

6. Changes
We may update these guidelines by posting a new version on this page.
