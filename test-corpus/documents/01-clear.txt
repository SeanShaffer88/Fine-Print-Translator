STREAMNEST SUBSCRIPTION TERMS

1. Free Trial
New members receive a 14-day free trial. At the end of the free trial, your membership will automatically convert to a paid subscription unless you cancel before the trial ends.

2. Renewal
Your subscription will automatically renew for successive periods of one month until you cancel.

3. Price and Fees
The subscription fee is $9.99 per month, charged to your payment method on each renewal date. We may change the subscription fee by giving you at least 30 days' notice by email.

4. Cancellation
You may cancel at any time in Account Settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

5. Refunds
Payments are non-refundable, and we do not provide refunds for partially used billing periods.

6. Contact
Questions about these terms can be sent to support@streamnest.example.
