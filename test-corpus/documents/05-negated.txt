TrailMap Season Pass Terms

1. No trial
There is no free trial for the Season Pass.

2. Term
The Season Pass lasts for 12 months from the date of purchase and does not renew automatically. To continue after it expires, you must buy a new pass.

3. Price
The Season Pass costs a one-time payment of $49.00. There are no additional fees.

4. Cancellation
Because the pass does not renew, there is nothing to cancel. You may close your account at any time in Settings.

5. Refunds
You may request a refund within 30 days of purchase if you have not downloaded any maps.
